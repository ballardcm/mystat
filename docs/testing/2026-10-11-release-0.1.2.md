## Observed live results

1. Before checking, the candidate displayed version 0.1.1 and `5s refresh`. Launch at login was checked; automatic update checks were unchecked. The automatic-check preference was absent, inheriting the bundle's disabled default.
2. A manual check against `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ballardcm/mystat/main/docs/appcast.xml` offered 0.1.2 and displayed the expected embedded release notes. Closing the offer deferred installation; the old version remained runnable.
3. Enabling automatic checks in the gear menu displayed a checkmark. After quitting, `SUEnableAutomaticChecks = 1` and `interval = 5` were persisted.
4. To exercise a due background check without waiting 24 hours, only the guest app's `SULastCheckTime` was changed from `2026-10-12 04:10:11 +0000` to `2026-10-10 04:10:11 +0000` while MyStat was stopped. The system clock, feed, and configured 86,400-second interval were unchanged. Relaunching produced a background 0.1.2 offer with “Remind Me Later” without another manual check. This validates scheduling with a simulated due time, not a 24-hour soak.
5. From that offer, “Install Update” downloaded the production release; “Install and Relaunch” replaced and restarted the app without an administrator or password prompt. The new bundle reported 0.1.2/build 3. Deep, strict code-signature verification passed, and exactly one MyStat process ran at the original installation path.
6. The new dropdown displayed version 0.1.2 and preserved `5s refresh`. A manual check reported that MyStat 0.1.2 was the newest version available.
7. Turning automatic checks off removed its checkmark. MyStat was quit and its process absence verified before rebooting the guest, avoiding reliance on restoring a previously running app.
8. After reboot, the guest automatically logged in and MyStat launched through its existing login-item registration. Exactly one MyStat process ran at the original path. The dropdown still displayed 0.1.2 and `5s refresh`; launch at login remained checked and automatic checks remained unchecked. Guest preferences confirmed `SUEnableAutomaticChecks = 0`, `interval = 5`, and a real post-check timestamp of `2026-10-12 04:12:20 +0000`.

The VM and MyStat 0.1.2 were left running with automatic checks off.
